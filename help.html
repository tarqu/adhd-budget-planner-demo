Your planner, on your phone and your computer

Short answers. You do not need to be technical for any of this.

Put it on your Home Screen

The planner is a web page, so it arrives in your browser rather than from an app store. Giving it an icon is not only for looks. A browser clears what a website saved after a while of not opening it, and it leaves a Home Screen app alone. Do this even if you only ever use one device.

iPhone and iPad

Open the planner in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Android

Open it in Chrome, tap the three dot menu, then Add to Home Screen or Install app.

Mac

Open it in Safari, then in the menu bar choose File, then Add to Dock.

Windows

Open it in Chrome or Edge, then in the menu choose Install page as app.

If the planner opened inside the Etsy app or your email app, tap the menu and choose Open in browser first. You cannot add it to your Home Screen from inside those apps.

Where your numbers live

Everything you type stays in the browser on that device. There is no account, no email and no cloud. Nobody at the shop can see your numbers. That also means each device keeps its own copy, and clearing your browser data clears the planner too.

Keep a backup

Open Settings inside the planner and choose Back up. It saves a small file of your numbers. Restore brings them back on the same device or on a new one. Worth doing after you first set everything up and after any big change.

Backup export is part of the full version. In the free demo the button explains this and your numbers stay saved in that browser meanwhile.

It works without internet

Once the planner has opened on a device, it keeps working with no connection. That is what the Home Screen icon is for.
